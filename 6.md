# Thai Massage Course STEP 6 Technique (61-76)

> 🌿 **Part A 61-64 · Pressure techniques for the back to relief blockages** — 0:00  
> After we have massaged his right side, we ask the recipient to lie face down in a prone position. We support the body with cushions if needed.

🅐 **61 · Heel press on the 2 Sen lines of the back** — 0:35  
With our heels sideways on the muscles on either side of the spine, we start pressing at the height of T1. With the use of our body weight, we sink slowly into the pressure, keeping our arms straight. We also release the pressure slowly. We adjust the rhythm of the pressure while the recipient exhales. Following the lines to the end of the sacrum, we reduce the pressure to half while massaging the sacrum.


🅐 **62 · Thumbs circle on the 2 lines** — 2:27  
With both thumbs, we describe three circles on the muscles on either side of the spine. On the third circle, we stretch outwards. While massaging, we feel for any knotted tissue. We massage until the end of the sacrum, reducing the pressure to 50% on the sacrum.


🅐 **63 · Thumbs press on the 2 lines** — 4:17  
With both thumbs facing, we press on the muscles on either side of the spine using our body weight, keeping our arms straight. Slowly increase the pressure, hold for about three seconds and release slowly. We massage until the end of the sacrum, applying only half of the pressure on the sacrum.


🅐 **64 · Sacrum energy press** — 5:21  
Double palms pressing gently on the curve of the sacrum. Hold for 5 seconds.

> 🌿 **Part B 65-70 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the back, hip and the back of the thigh** — 5:39

🅑 **65 · Thumb press on the inner Sen line** — 5:42  
We massage with facing thumbs touching on the left side next to the spine. Start at the height of T1 until the end of the sacrum. In a kneeling position with straight arms, we sink slowly into the pressure, holding for about three seconds and releasing slowly, adjusting to the breathing rhythm of the recipient. At the lumbar region, we switch to cross-thumb pressing and continue until the end of the sacrum. Apply only 50% pressure on the sacrum. We massage back upwards the same way. During Thai massage practice, we develop a deep and slow breath and a smooth massage rhythm, maintaining a steady and relaxed awareness. Again, we massage downwards until the end of the sacrum.


🅑 **66 · Hip massage: 5 techniques** — 9:05  
1. Elbow press: with the flat upper part of the elbow, we slowly increase the pressure and hold.
2. Elbow circle: we maintain the pressure and describe circles on the muscled area.
3. Arm pressure: with the flat side of the forearm and the use of our body weight.
4. Arm circle: applied on the muscled area of the hip.
5. Arm roll: performed outwards to stretch the muscles.


🅑 **67 · Heel press on the back of the leg** — 10:51  
We massage from underneath the buttocks downwards. Skip the knee crease and apply less pressure on the calves.


🅑 **68 · Arm roll on the sole** — 11:26  
While we support the foot half kneeling, we perform arm rolls on the sole.


🅑 **69 · Foot press on the back of the thigh** — 11:55  
Through shifting our body weight, holding the foot above the ankle joint, we commence above the knee crease. From the second step onwards, we simultaneously pull a bit at the foot.


🅑 **70 · Lower leg stretch** — 12:24  
By pushing the dorsal towards the buttocks. The foot tip is tucked into the knee crease.


🅑 **65b · Thumb press on the inner Sen line** — 12:36  
We move to sit on the right side of the recipient to massage his right side, repeating techniques 65 to 70. Working on the inner Sen lines on either side of the spine balances the energy flow and is very beneficial for lower back pain. The course of the Sen lines continues on the inner thighs, therefore we can also concentrate to work the inner thighs as we have done with the leg massage.

> 🌿 **Part C 71-76 · Pressure techniques for the spine and leg; Relaxing techniques** — 19:18

🅒 **71 · Plantar flex** — 19:21  
Both heels are pushing on the balls of the feet towards the buttocks.


🅒 **72 · Crossed lower leg stretch** — 19:34  
Place the legs a bit apart and push slowly with the use of your body weight. Then switch legs.


🅒 **73 · Cobra spinal stretch** — 19:54  
With our body weight resting on our heels, we kneel below the buttocks. We grab each other's wrists and slowly lean back while the recipient exhales.


🅒 **74 · Pelvis stretch** — 20:13  
By lifting the legs up while one foot is loosely placed on the sacrum.


🅒 **75 · Elephant walk** — 20:28  
We walk heel pressing sideways on the muscles on either side of the spine, all the way down the back towards the ankle joints.


🅒 **76 · Standing on the sole** — 21:26  
With our heels, shifting our weight from one side to the other.
