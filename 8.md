# Thai Massage Course STEP 8 Technique (85-98)

> 🌿 **Part A 85-86 · Stretching techniques for the upper body and the sides** — [0:00](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=0)

🅐 **85 · Side twist** — [0:04](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=4)  
One foot lightly on the thigh, the knee turned inwards against the hip next to it. The palm pushing forward, the other hand placed in front above the elbow, pushing backwards. Switch side.


🅐 **86 · Chest widener** — [0:28](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=28)  
Through pushing with the thigh on the muscle between the spine and scapula, simultaneously pull on the arms a bit upwards and backwards, more pressing than drawing.

---

> 🌿 **Part B 87-90 · Facial lines; Head lines** — [0:44](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=44)

🅑 **87 · Facial lines** — [0:47](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=47)  
Before touching the face, we clean our hands. We support the head and back with our body and slide with the thumbs on the facial lines from the center outwards. The chin line complete with three circles on the jaw.


🅑 **88 · Spider jump** — [1:42](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=102)  
Dynamically grabbing with the fingertips on the scalp for a stimulating effect to the nerves.


🅑 **89 · Sagittal line** — [2:10](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=130)  
With facing thumbs, we commence pressing from the base of the skull towards the hairline. Stand up for it.


🅑 **90 · Coronal line** — [2:39](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=159)  
Start pressing on the crown point and continue simultaneously thumb pressing towards the ears. Apply less pressure on the temporalis.

---

> 🌿 **Part C 91-95 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the neck and shoulder** — [3:02](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=182)

🅒 **91 · 5 Energy points** — [3:05](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=185)  
Pressing with the thumb, pull a little upwards and hold for five seconds at each point. Point 1: center of the lower skull's rim. Point 2: about one thumb width to the left. Point 3: another thumb width to the left. Point 4: one thumb width from point 1 to the right. Point 5: another thumb width to the right.


🅒 **92 · Neck squeeze with thumbs** — [3:46](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=226)  
We bend his head a little downwards and interlock our hands with both thumbs pointing down. We press on the external muscles on either side of the spine, walking downwards.


🅒 **93 · Neck squeeze with heels** — [4:40](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=280)  
With the heels of our hands, we are pressing on the muscles downwards. We bring the head again in a straight position.


🅒 **94 · Arm rolls on the shoulder** — [5:15](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=315)  
With the flat side of our forearms placed on top of the shoulders, we sink into the pressure and roll outwards.


🅒 **95 · Elbow pivot lever** — [5:33](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=333)  
Holding the wrist of the recipient, we press our elbow on the top muscle of the shoulder. Meanwhile we draw at his wrist to a backward direction. At the same time we support lifting his elbow backwards with our other hand to increase the pressure and the stretch. Release simultaneously, then massage the right side.

---

> 🌿 **Part D 96-98 · Closing** — [6:50](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=410)

🅓 **96 · Soft stretch forward** — [6:54](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=414)  
We place our palms on the upper back and push gently and slowly forward. The recipient can also straighten his legs.


🅓 **97 · Relaxing heel press** — [7:07](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=427)  
The recipient locks his hands in front of him on the floor. Lightly heel pressing the muscles on the left side of the spine while the other hand supports on the shoulder. Massage the same way on the right side.


🅓 **98 · Finger tip percussion** — [7:45](https://youtu.be/UIY_rKItO4E?t=465)  
With both hands loosely together, fingers apart, we clap with our fingertips on the muscle area. The movement comes out of the wrists, our elbows are pointing outwards.
