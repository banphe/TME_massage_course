# Thai Massage Course STEP 5 Technique (49-60)

> 🌿 **Part A 49-55 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the arm, shoulder blade and back** — 0:00  
> after completing the abdominal massage we transfer the recipient into the sideline position his left side up

🅐 **49 · Arm extension** — 0:24  
Technic 49 arm extension press to outward


🅐 **50 · Palm press on the outer arm** — 0:33  
Technic 50 Palm press on the outer arm from the upper arm to the lower arm and Back Again skip the elbow and finish with another arm extension


🅐 **51 · Triangle position arm stretch** — 1:11  
Technic 51 triangle position arm stretch by pressing above the elbow and pushing the hip his fingertips pointing downwards


🅐 **52 · Two hands pulling the Trapezius** — 1:27  
Technic 52 two hands pulling the trapezius pulling in a backward direction as we lean our body backwards


🅐 **53 · Thumb press on the scapula** — 2:05  
Technic 53 some press on his scapula with one hand we describe an amp pattern with our thumb pressing from below the inner angle of the scapular spine towards the lower tip of the scapula simultaneously we pull a bit the shoulder towards us with our other hand and release at the same time


🅐 **54 · Thumb press along the outer border of the scapula** — 2:36  
Technic 54 some press along the outer border of the scapula we simultaneously pulling on the shoulder towards us to increase pressure from the upper inner angle to the lower tip at the lower tip we switch hands and continue thumb pressing to below the armpit but no more pulling on the shoulder


🅐 **55 · The outer Sen line of the back** — 3:06  
Technic 55 the outer sand line of the back we commence at the height of T1 pressing with facing thumbs touching on the outer send line about one thumb width apart of the spine to stay in line we use the aid of the grubbing thumb at the lumbar region we switched to cross some pressing to intensify the pressure at the sacrum region we would use the pressure to 50 percent and massage to the end of the sacrum

> 🌿 **Part B 56-60 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the hip, leg and sole of the foot; Spinal twist** — 4:38

🅑 **56 · Hip points** — 4:41  
Technic 56 hit points we press with facing sounds touching on three points


🅑 **57 · Palm press on the flexed leg** — 5:03  
Technic 57 Palm press on the flex black commands on the Outer tie with one hand supporting on the knee switch hands to Palm press the lower leg and proceed the same way up


🅑 **58 · Butterfly press on the straightened leg** — 5:48  
Technic 58 butterfly pressing on the straightened leg we start at the inner thigh downwards with heels pressing sideways skip the name


🅑 **59 · Arm roll and elbow press on the sole** — 6:14  
Technic 59 arm roll and elbow press on the sole therefore we move to sit underneath the straightened lower leg placing the sole facing up


🅑 **58b · Butterfly press on the straightened leg** — 6:48  
after that we continue with the previous technique to massage butterfly pressing the straightened leg upwards


🅑 **60 · Spinal twist** — 7:15  
Technic 60 spinal twist one hand locks the knee while the other hand is slowly pushing the shoulder downwards the recipient lies again in the Supine position and we proceed to massage his right arm practicing the technique 37 to 47. and practice the techniques 49-60 on his right side which is a mirror image of the left side
