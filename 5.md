# Thai Massage Course STEP 5 Technique (49-60)

> 🌿 **Part A 49-55 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the arm, shoulder blade and back** — 0:00  
> After completing the abdominal massage, we transfer the recipient into the sideline position, his left side up.

🅐 **49 · Arm extension** — 0:24  
Arm extension, pressed outward.


🅐 **50 · Palm press on the outer arm** — 0:33  
From the upper arm to the lower arm and back again. Skip the elbow and finish with another arm extension.


🅐 **51 · Triangle position arm stretch** — 1:11  
By pressing above the elbow and pushing the hip, his fingertips pointing downwards.


🅐 **52 · Two hands pulling the Trapezius** — 1:27  
Pulling in a backward direction as we lean our body backwards.


🅐 **53 · Thumb press on the scapula** — 2:05  
With one hand, we describe an arc pattern with our thumb, pressing from below the inner angle of the scapular spine towards the lower tip of the scapula. Simultaneously, we pull the shoulder a bit towards us with our other hand, and release at the same time.


🅐 **54 · Thumb press along the outer border of the scapula** — 2:36  
While simultaneously pulling the shoulder towards us to increase pressure, thumb press from the upper inner angle to the lower tip. At the lower tip, switch hands and continue thumb pressing to below the armpit, but no more pulling on the shoulder.


🅐 **55 · The outer Sen line of the back** — 3:06  
We commence at the height of T1, pressing with facing thumbs on the outer Sen line, about one thumb width apart from the spine. To stay in line, we use the aid of the guiding thumb. At the lumbar region, we switch to cross-thumb pressing to intensify the pressure. At the sacrum region, we reduce the pressure to 50% and massage to the end of the sacrum.

> 🌿 **Part B 56-60 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the hip, leg and sole of the foot; Spinal twist** — 4:38

🅑 **56 · Hip points** — 4:41  
We press with facing thumbs on three points.


🅑 **57 · Palm press on the flexed leg** — 5:03  
Commence on the outer thigh with one hand supporting on the knee. Switch hands to palm press the lower leg and proceed the same way up.


🅑 **58 · Butterfly press on the straightened leg** — 5:48  
We start at the inner thigh downwards, with heels pressing sideways. Skip the knee.


🅑 **59 · Arm roll and elbow press on the sole** — 6:14  
For this, we move to sit underneath the straightened lower leg, placing the sole facing up.


🅑 **58b · Butterfly press on the straightened leg** — 6:48  
After that, we continue with the previous technique, butterfly pressing the straightened leg upwards.


🅑 **60 · Spinal twist** — 7:15  
One hand locks the knee while the other hand is slowly pushing the shoulder downwards. The recipient lies again in the supine position. We proceed to massage the right arm, practicing techniques 37 to 47, then techniques 49-60 on the right side, which is a mirror image of the left side.
