# Thai Massage Course STEP 3 Technique (37-47)

> 🌿 **Part A 37-41 · Pressure techniques for the inner and outer arm** — 0:00  
> after we have massaged the right leg we proceed to the left arm Technic 37 Palm walk on the inside arm

🅐 **37 · Palm walk on the inside arm** — 0:26  
commencing on the straight arm above the wrist we skip the elbow crease and continue to below the armpit gentle massage with the use of our body weight keeping our arms straight


🅐 **38 · Line 1 inner arm** — 1:03  
Technic 38 Line 1 inner arm with side thumbs pressing from above the wrist to below the elbow crease on an imaginary extended line of the middle finger continue from above the elbow crease with heel pressing


🅐 **39 · Line 2 inner arm** — 1:38  
Technic 39 line 2 inner arm with double thumbs pressing against line two an imaginary extension of the little finger skip the elbow crease and turn the Palm downwards to stabilized biceps and proceed with cross-sump pressing on the upper arm


🅐 **40 · Line 1 outer arm** — 2:27  
Technic 40 line 1 outer arm Flex the arm and move to knee along the outer arm with some pressing from above the wrist to below the elbow on an imaginary extended line of the middle finger for the upper arm use heel pressing sideways


🅐 **41 · Line 2 outer arm** — 3:03  
Technic 41 line 2 outer arm pressing diagonal from above the wrist to below the elbow imagine an extended line of the little finger skip the elbow and bend the arm the elbow pointing up at the same time some pressing line two on the upper arm and pulling with the supporting hand to increase pressure

> 🌿 **Part B 42-47 · Hand massage** — 3:53

🅑 **42 · Squeezing palm and fingers** — 3:56  
Technic 42 squeezing palm and fingers holding the hand with the Palm showing towards us we squeeze the hand with both thumbs from the root to the fingertips support the fingers underneath


🅑 **43 · Finger massage** — 4:22  
Technic 43 finger massage turn the hand that the dorsal is pointing up starting with the thumb we massage gentle all fingers skipping the joints switch hand at the middle finger


🅑 **44 · Soft pulling on the finger tips** — 4:57  
Technic 44 soft pulling of the fingertips


🅑 **45 · Metacarpal stretch** — 5:01  
Technic 45 metacarpal stretch by pressing and pulling with our fingertips above the joints


🅑 **46 · Wrist and finger stretch** — 5:10  
Technic 46 wrist and finger stretch in a 90 degree angle


🅑 **47 · Dorsal stretch** — 5:18  
Technic 47 dorsal stretch with both heels sliding firmly outwards
