# Thai Massage Course STEP 3 Technique (37-47)

> 🌿 **Part A 37-41 · Pressure techniques for the inner and outer arm** — 0:00  
> After we have massaged the right leg, we proceed to the left arm. Technique 37: palm walk on the inside arm.

🅐 **37 · Palm walk on the inside arm** — 0:26  
Commencing on the straight arm above the wrist, we skip the elbow crease and continue to below the armpit. Gentle massage with the use of our body weight, keeping our arms straight.


🅐 **38 · Line 1 inner arm** — 1:03  
With the sides of the thumbs, pressing from above the wrist to below the elbow crease on an imaginary extended line of the middle finger. Continue from above the elbow crease with heel pressing.


🅐 **39 · Line 2 inner arm** — 1:38  
With double thumbs pressing against line 2, an imaginary extension of the little finger. Skip the elbow crease and turn the palm downwards to stabilize the biceps, then proceed with cross-thumb pressing on the upper arm.


🅐 **40 · Line 1 outer arm** — 2:27  
Flex the arm and move to kneel along the outer arm. Thumb pressing from above the wrist to below the elbow on an imaginary extended line of the middle finger. For the upper arm, use heel pressing sideways.


🅐 **41 · Line 2 outer arm** — 3:03  
Pressing diagonally from above the wrist to below the elbow, along an imaginary extended line of the little finger. Skip the elbow and bend the arm, the elbow pointing up. At the same time, thumb pressing line 2 on the upper arm and pulling with the supporting hand to increase pressure.

> 🌿 **Part B 42-47 · Hand massage** — 3:53

🅑 **42 · Squeezing palm and fingers** — 3:56  
Holding the hand with the palm showing towards us, we squeeze the hand with both thumbs from the root to the fingertips. Support the fingers underneath.


🅑 **43 · Finger massage** — 4:22  
Turn the hand so the dorsal is pointing up. Starting with the thumb, we massage gently all fingers, skipping the joints. Switch hand at the middle finger.


🅑 **44 · Soft pulling on the finger tips** — 4:57  
Soft pulling of the fingertips.


🅑 **45 · Metacarpal stretch** — 5:01  
By pressing and pulling with our fingertips above the joints.


🅑 **46 · Wrist and finger stretch** — 5:10  
In a 90 degree angle.


🅑 **47 · Dorsal stretch** — 5:18  
With both heels sliding firmly outwards.
