# Thai Massage Course STEP 2 Technique (6-36)

> 🌿 **Part A 6-13 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the flexed leg** — 0:00

🅐 **6 · Palm press on line 1+2, inner leg** — 0:05  
We flex the left leg, our supporting hand placed on the right thigh. With the use of our body weight, we palm press the inner left thigh, keeping our arms straight. We stop before the knee and palm press back. If needed, support the leg with a cushion.


🅐 **7 · Butterfly press** — 1:05  
Both heels are pressing on line 1 and line 2 simultaneously, towards the knee and back. Support with a cushion if needed.


🅐 **8 · Foot press on line 3, inner leg** — 1:36  
The outer foot blocks the flexed front thigh. The inner foot is pressing with the sole against the back of the thigh. Start above the knee crease and stop before the groin, then foot press back. We hold the foot and support the descent of the straight leg. For more pressure, use the heel or the edge of your foot.


🅐 **9 · Pulling the front thigh** — 2:12  
Alternately, we walk pulling with the fingers on the muscle at the height of line 2, towards the groin and back. One or both feet are tucked between the flexed legs.


🅐 **10 · Two hands pull** — 2:46  
Both hands progressively placed on top of each other on the front thigh, for more traction.


🅐 **11 · Heel press on line 2, inner leg** — 3:26  
At the same time, we draw the knee a bit towards us. From above the knee crease upwards and back again.


🅐 **12 · Outside push** — 3:58  
Half kneeling, his foot placed against our groin. We support the knee with one hand. We move forward with our body, pushing his leg outwards. Simultaneously we palm press his inner thigh. Repeat pushing and pressing up to the groin and back. Block the straight leg with your foot if it moves up.


🅐 **13 · Heel walk on tight tendon** — 4:31  
His lower leg placed on our foot crease, we bring his legs straightened outward. We palm walk on the inner thigh towards the knee crease and back.

> 🌿 **Part B 14-17 · Pressure techniques for the bended leg** — 5:09

🅑 **14 · Pulling the calf** — 5:13  
Pulling the calf slowly with both hands. Feels good at the lower back.


🅑 **15 · Dragging the calf muscles** — 5:25  
From the middle of the back side towards the sides, alternately walking with all fingers downwards and up again.


🅑 **16 · Thigh squeeze** — 5:51  
With interlaced hands, walking upwards and back. We are pressing on inside line 1 and outside line 2.


🅑 **17 · Thumb press on line 1 outside lower leg** — 6:29  
Directly next to the shin bone, pressing downwards, while simultaneously pulling above the knee towards us.

> 🌿 **Part C 18-22 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the raised leg** — 6:58

🅒 **18 · Lower leg stretch** — 7:02  
By pressing on the dorsal.


🅒 **19 · Arm roll on the back of the thigh** — 7:10  
With the outer forearm towards the buttocks and back. The outer hand supports the foot.


🅒 **20 · Heel press with additional stretching** — 7:38  
Heel press on the back of the thigh with additional stretching — pushing and pressing at the same time.


🅒 **21 · Foot press against the back of the thigh** — 8:10  
Foot press against the back of the thigh, while simultaneously gently pulling the foot.


🅒 **22 · Lower back relief** — 8:27  
Toes and ball of the foot placed at the lower pelvic edge. We lean back to straighten the leg, holding at the ankle and dorsal. The hip moves up onto our toes.

> 🌿 **Part D 23-28 · Pressure techniques using the forearm and elbow** — 9:00

🅓 **23 · Arm roll on the front thigh** — 9:03  
We move to sit between his thighs, his legs placed over ours. Starting over the knee, working up until before the groin and back again. We lean into the pressure with the flat side of our forearm and perform arm rolls upwards. We increase the pressure in the second round.


🅓 **24 · Arm roll on the inner thigh** — 9:51  
We flex the leg a bit outwards and perform the arm roll from above the knee crease upwards and back again.


🅓 **25 · Elbow slide on the outer thigh** — 10:39  
On the straightened leg, we place our elbow at the posterior part of the vastus lateralis, the muscle of the outer thigh. Holding the pressure, we slide towards the front thigh. Start from above the knee upwards and back again.


🅓 **26 · Arm roll on the inner lower leg** — 11:37  
We modify our sitting position to support the lower leg and expose the inner calf. With our lower forearm, we start performing the arm roll from below the knee crease until the lower part of the sole and back.


🅓 **27 · Elbow press beside the shin bone** — 12:12  
Directly next to the outer tibial crest, we press with the tip of our elbow downwards and up again. With the fingertips of our other hand placed around the tibial crest, we guide the elbow press.


🅓 **28 · Elbow slide on the outer lower leg** — 13:12  
We bend the foot a bit to expose the outer muscles of the lower leg, so we can press with the elbow sliding towards the front. Start below the fibular head, downwards and up again.

> 🌿 **Part E 29-33 · Pressure and stretching techniques for the leaning leg** — 14:26

🅔 **29 · Palm press on the leaning outer leg** — 14:30  
We bend and lean the leg against a straight leg, supporting with a cushion at knee height and with our foot tucked against his foot. Both hands are palm pressing the lower and upper leg towards the knee and out again.


🅔 **30 · Crossed thumb press on line 3, outer leg** — 15:04  
We start on line 3, behind and above the ankle joint, until below the fibular head. Skipping the knee, we continue above the knee crease until below the greater trochanter.


🅔 **31 · Heel press on line 3, outer leg** — 15:44  
The lower hand supports the knee. The upper hand is heel pressing on line 3 towards the knee. Switch hands and continue heel pressing with the lower hand towards the ankle joint.


🅔 **32 · Upper leg stretch** — 16:11  
Our thigh and hand supports his knee. His lower leg is flexed towards his upper body. We heel press on line 2, stretching away from us in a few steps. Stop before the knee.


🅔 **33 · Lower leg stretch** — 16:34  
By pushing the knee downwards, support at the heel.

> 🌿 **Part F 34-36 · Spinal twist; Stretching the entire leg** — 16:47

🅕 **34 · Spinal Twist** — 16:52  
While the recipient exhales, push his knee downwards slowly and hold at the shoulder.


🅕 **35 · Stretching the front of the leg** — 17:06  
Half kneeling, we support the leg, the heel placed in our palm against our forearm. The other hand is heel pressing from above the knee upwards. Our body leans in at the same time.


🅕 **36 · Stretching the back of the leg** — 17:27  
Through heel pressing simultaneously on the dorsal of the foot and the front thigh, with an additional push towards the groin — release at the same time. Repeat techniques 6 to 36 on the right leg.
