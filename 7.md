# Thai Massage Course STEP 7 Technique (77-84)

💆 **77 · Knee press on the back of the thigh** — [0:06](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=6)  
We ask the recipient to turn around to lay on his back. As we shift our body weight into the pressure on the flexed leg, we lift our foot off the floor. One hand supports the knee while the shoulder supports the straightened leg.


💆 **78 · 13 points on the right sole** — [1:00](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=60)  
We support a straightened leg with our body and press with the point of the elbow the points on the sole. The points between the toes we press with the thumb. If the recipient is much taller than the masseur, adjust the body support by stepping backwards and the pressure can be done only with thumb.


💆 **79 · Alternative heel press on the back of the thigh** — [2:41](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=161)  
Use this technique when the masseur is much taller than the recipient and when you massage a very flexible person.


💆 **78b · 13 points on the left sole** — [3:16](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=196)  
This is a very effective stretch of the posterior leg and the front of the lower leg in combination with a reflexology massage.


💆 **80 · Kneeing the back of both thighs with an additional stretch** — [4:36](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=276)  
With our knees pointing inwards, we press from above the knee crease towards the buttocks and back. From the second knee press onwards, we are simultaneously pushing his heels forward for an additional stretch to work on shortened hamstrings or piriformis problems.


💆 **81 · Shaking the legs** — [5:29](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=329)  
Holding the legs, our gentle walking body movement generates this relaxation for the lower back, always applicable after a demanding stretch.


💆 **82 · Lower leg stretch** — [5:54](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=354)  
The soles are placed on our patellas, our hands on the recipient's knees.


💆 **83 · Crossed legs lower body stretch** — [6:08](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=368)  
With his dorsals placed below our knees, our hands on his knees, we apply less pressure on his upper dorsal, then switch legs.


💆 **84 · Crossed legs back stretch** — [6:28](https://youtu.be/Wq5XESFwHB0?t=388)  
With his dorsals placed below our knees, we grasp each other's wrists. Through leaning back we lift his upper body up and hold for a few seconds. Repeat a second time, step back and bring him into a sitting position.
