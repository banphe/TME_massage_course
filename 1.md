# Thai Massage Course STEP 1 Technique (1-5)

> 🌿 **Start Step 1** — 0:00  
> Welcome to TME massage training. This whole body massage of 90 minutes duration shows a complex sequence of pressing and stretching techniques, each of them with its own specific effect to counteract shortened muscles and to balance the life force energy levels in the Sen lines. Detailed footage and descriptions of every individual technique and the division in eight steps makes learning easy.

---

💆 **1 · Dorsal Stretch** — 0:33  
Dorsal stretch, with palms pressing downwards.


💆 **2+3 · Warming Up the Feet and Legs; Patella Circles** — 0:39  
Technique 2:  
Warming up the feet and legs. We begin with alternating palm press on the inner border of the feet, skipping the ankle joint and the knee. We walk up the legs and down again. We work with straight arms, gently leaning into the pressure.  
Technique 3:  
Patella circles. With our palms placed on the patellas, we perform gentle circles inwards without applying any pressure. Then we continue palming the lower legs downwards.


💆 **4/6 · Points at the Inner Border of the Foot and Heel Press on Lower Inside Leg** — 2:27  
6 points at the inner border of the foot, then heel press on the lower inside leg. Thumb press from underneath the big toe to the heel under the bone, followed by heel pressing up the muscles of the lower leg and back again.


💆 **5 · Heel Press on Line 1 of the Outer Lower Leg** — 4:07  
We press directly next to the shin bone, from underneath its head to above the ankle. The soles are pressing against our front thigh.
